HW 6 - Snowman: Bump + Normal Mapping

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
The snowman faces right; the forest and ground use your two JPEGs.
The snowman hops as the forest and ground scroll at different speeds.
Animated snowflakes also update the forest texture itself.
The two white spheres use bump mapping; the carrot nose uses a normal map.
Uncheck Surface Detail to compare mapped versus ordinary lighting.
Pause Movement stops both hopping and scenery so you can inspect the scene.
Drag with the mouse to rotate the snowman.
Use the arrow keys and w, s keys to move the camera.
Use the slider and dropdown to rotate the whole snowman; the button starts/stops spinning.
